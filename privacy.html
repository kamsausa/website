Privacy notice

Last updated: [DATE]

The Korean American Medical Student Association (KAMSA), which operates under the Korean American Medical Association (KAMA), collects a small amount of information to run its membership program. This notice explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider; KAMSA cannot see it).
  • Profile details: school, graduation year, current status, regional chapter, specialty interests, and optionally a school email, LinkedIn link, and profile photo.
  • Choices you make: whether to be listed in the member directory, and when you agreed to this notice.

How we use it

  • To review and approve membership, and to contact you about KAMSA programs.
  • To connect you with your regional chapter. Chapter leads may receive your name, email, school, and class year.
  • To show your profile in the member directory, only if you opt in. The directory is visible only to approved, signed-in members.

We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers. Partner organizations never receive member lists.

Where it is stored

Member data is stored with our database and authentication provider (Supabase). Access is limited to the national board members who manage membership.

Your choices

  • Turn your directory listing on or off anytime under My profile in the member portal.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your account and data by emailing kamsa.usa@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this notice: kamsa.usa@gmail.com.
