Membership

What you get as a KAMSA member

Membership is free and open to Korean American pre-medical students, medical students, and alumni nationwide.

01

A regional chapter

Access to your local chapter's events, mentorship, and peer community.

02

Membership directory

Opt in to connect with members across the country by school, region, or specialty interest.

03

Partner discounts

Member-only offers from partners like JAANUU and StudyRemote.

04

Mentorship

Guidance from the wider Korean American medical community, including KAMA physicians.

05

Leadership experience

Chapter and national board roles that build real organizational experience.

06

The KAMSA Newsletter

A periodic roundup of chapter news, advocacy, research, and member highlights.
