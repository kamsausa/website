How it works

  1. STEP 1

    Name one need, one offer

    One connection you're looking for, and one thing you can give another student. Specific beats broad.

  2. STEP 2

    We place you in a small group

    KAMSA uses its national network to form intentional groups by specialty, city, or training level.

  3. STEP 3

    You leave with a contact

    Participants who consent join a post-session contact directory, and can request a mentor introduction.
