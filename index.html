Who we are

A professional home for the next generation of physicians.

KAMSA brings Korean American medical students together across schools, specialties, and regions—creating a national community where members can learn, lead, and belong.

Through regional chapters, physician mentorship, and a national convention, we help students build relationships that support them throughout medical school and beyond.

01

Support chapters

Equip regional leaders to host service, networking, and educational programs rooted in their communities.

02

Build mentorship

Connect students with Korean American physicians through our relationship with KAMA and the broader medical community.

03

Gather nationally

Bring students and physicians together for an annual convention centered on learning, connection, and growth.
