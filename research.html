Research

The KAMSA Research Network

Connecting KAMSA members with Korean American and Asian-heritage physician-researchers for faculty-mentored projects — with clear scopes, defined roles, and a real deliverable at the end.

Announcement — applications are not open yet. Watch this page and the newsletter for the intake date.

What the network does

01

Builds projects with physician-researchers

We work with Korean American and other Asian-heritage researchers to develop new projects — or to open seats on projects already underway.

02

Pairs mentorship with real involvement

Members join research through mentorship from faculty who are actively doing the work — not observation, but assigned tasks and named contributions.

03

Forms teams around shared interests

Students group by focus — specialty-based, public health, AI, quality improvement, medical education — and collaborate on faculty-mentored work.

How to get involved

Two tracks. Faculty proposals are reviewed and approved by program leadership before any project opens to student applicants.
