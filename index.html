Who we are

One community for students training across the country

KAMSA brings Korean American medical students together across schools, specialties, and regions so they can learn, lead, and belong.

Regional chapters, a national community, events, and programming keep students connected from preclinical years through residency applications and beyond.

Chapter funding

KAMSA Micro-Grant

Limited funding for regional chapters — up to $150 per request, one request per chapter per academic year — to help cover events and programming: speaker panels, mentorship socials, community health outreach, and others.

Request funding Ask a question
  1. STEP 1 Plan your event and estimate the budget
  2. STEP 2 Submit the request form at least four weeks ahead
  3. STEP 3 The national board reviews and confirms by email
  4. STEP 4 Hold the event, then send receipts and a short recap
